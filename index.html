Higgins

Higgins is a private, single-household assistant. It runs on its owner's own computer and is not offered to the public.

With the owner's permission, Higgins reads the owner's Gmail and Google Calendar (read-only) to keep track of receipts, warranties, appointments and household upkeep. It never sends, changes or deletes mail or calendar events.

Higgins also sends text messages about home service visits and household updates, from +1 650 668 4450, only to people who have opted in by texting START to that number and replying YES to confirm. How to get texts from Higgins.
