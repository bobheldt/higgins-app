Higgins text message terms

Last updated: October 30, 2026

The program

Higgins is the private, automated home assistant for the Heldt household, acting for Bob Heldt. From +1 650 668 4450 it sends text messages about home service visits (scheduling, confirmations and reminders), household bill updates and requests to approve a household task. It does not send marketing or promotional messages.

Who gets texts and how to opt in

Only members of the household and service providers the household has hired. You opt in by texting START to +1 650 668 4450. Higgins does not text a number that has not opted in. After you opt in, you get a confirmation message.

Frequency and cost

Message frequency varies with your appointments and requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Stopping texts and getting help

  • Reply STOP to stop all texts. You get one message confirming you are unsubscribed, and no more after that. Reply START to opt back in.
  • Reply HELP for help, or email higgins.heldt@gmail.com.

Other terms

Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages. Your information is handled as described in the privacy policy; mobile information is never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
