Higgins privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Higgins is a private assistant used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it uses

Higgins requests read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It uses that data only to show the owner their own receipts, warranties, confirmations, appointments and household reminders.

Where the data goes

  • Google data is stored and processed on the owner's own computer.
  • Google data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else.
  • Data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads Google data.

Higgins's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Text messages (SMS)

Higgins sends text messages from +1 650 668 4450 only to people who have opted in by texting START to that number and replying YES to confirm: members of the owner's household and service providers the owner has hired. See the text message terms.

  • Phone numbers, messages and opt-in records are used only to send and keep track of those texts.
  • No mobile information will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.
  • Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.
  • Messages are delivered through the SMS provider Twilio, which handles them only to deliver them.
  • Reply STOP at any time to stop all texts. Opt-outs take effect right away.

Removing access

The owner can remove Higgins's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted from the owner's computer on request.
