Higgins privacy policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

Higgins is a private assistant used only by its owner's household. It is not offered to the public.

What Google data it uses

Higgins requests read-only access to Gmail and Google Calendar. It uses that data only to show the owner their own receipts, warranties, confirmations, appointments and household reminders.

Where the data goes

  • Data is stored and processed on the owner's own computer.
  • Data is never sold, never used for advertising and never shared with anyone else.
  • Data is not used to train general-purpose AI models.
  • No person other than the owner reads it.

Higgins's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The owner can remove Higgins's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Stored data is deleted from the owner's computer on request.

Contact

Questions can go to the support email shown on the Google sign-in screen for Higgins.
